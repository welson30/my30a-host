How to order groceries

Publix Watersound Town Center · Inlet Beach, FL

Important: Add items to your Publix cart but do not checkout on the Publix website. We do the shopping and delivery — you pay us, not Publix.
1
Open Publix Watersound
Tap the button below — opens directly at our local Publix, Watersound Town Center, Inlet Beach. Confirm the right store is selected.
2
Add items to your cart
Search any product. You'll see the real photo, name, size, and current Publix price. No account needed.
3
Cart → Review Order → View Details
Tap the cart icon → "Review Order" → "View Details" to see all items with photos and prices on one screen.
4
Screenshot & send to us
Screenshot that page and upload here, or share your cart list by email. We confirm the total + service fee and send a payment link.
5
We shop, pack & deliver
You pay via Stripe. We shop and stock your kitchen before check-in. Real receipt sent after — any price difference automatically refunded or charged.
