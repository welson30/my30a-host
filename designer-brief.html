The Big Idea

"You're not selling services.
You're selling a better life."

My30A Host is an AI-powered personal concierge for guests vacationing on the 30A coastal corridor in Florida — one of the most exclusive beach destinations in the US, home to Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach, WaterColor, and Seaside.

People don't come to 30A to manage logistics. They come to live — to feel the pace, the light, the freedom. Our product handles everything before they arrive so that nothing gets in the way of the experience they came for.

Better way to live

Real & local

Design Direction

✅ Do
  • ✓Large, full-bleed photos that stop the scroll
  • ✓Generous whitespace — luxury breathes
  • ✓Serif headlines, sans body — editorial contrast
  • ✓Gold as a sparingly used accent
  • ✓Warm tones throughout — cream, sand, ocean
  • ✓Minimal text — images do the selling
  • ✓Subtle hover animations, smooth transitions
  • ✓Cards with real photos, not icons
❌ Don't
  • ✗Flat, corporate design — this is not a SaaS dashboard
  • ✗Harsh shadows or borders — keep it soft
  • ✗Cluttered layouts — one focal point per section
  • ✗Too many colors — stick to the palette
  • ✗Small text — minimum 12px in the app
  • ✗Redesign the structure — polish what's there
  • ✗Generic stock photo vibe — photos must feel real
  • ✗Busy backgrounds with patterns or textures
🎯

Your Job — What To Polish

You are NOT designing from scratch. The prototype is fully built and live. Your job is to take what exists, elevate the visual quality, and deliver Figma files the developer can implement.
  • Refine typography consistency — sizes, weights, line heights across all screens
  • Improve spacing and breathing room — sections feel cramped in places
  • Create a component library — buttons, cards, inputs, badges, icons, nav elements
  • Polish photo cards — overlays, gradients, text positioning on images
  • Refine the app screens — especially the chat UI, service cards, and local guide
  • Improve mobile responsiveness — app is primarily a mobile experience
  • Add micro-interactions — hover states, button presses, card transitions
  • Deliver: Figma file + exported assets + component library + all screen specs
