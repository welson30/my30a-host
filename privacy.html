4. Payment & Stripe

All payments are processed through Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. When you pay for a service, your card information is entered directly on Stripe's secure platform. My30A Host never sees, stores, or has access to your credit card number, CVV, or full billing details.

For certain services (airport transfers, grocery delivery), we use Stripe's card hold feature — your card is authorized when you book, and charged only when we confirm the service. If we cannot provide the service, the hold is released immediately with no charge.

6. Grocery delivery — substitution policy

Our grocery service uses the Publix Watersound Town Center (11291 US Hwy 98 E, Inlet Beach, FL). When you build your cart on Publix's website, you see real photos, sizes, and current prices.

Important: Do not complete checkout on the Publix website. Your cart is used as a shopping list only. We shop, pay for the items, and deliver to your property. You pay us — not Publix directly.

Substitutions

If an item is out of stock at the time of shopping, we will contact you via the app or text message before purchasing a substitute. You have the right to approve or decline any substitution. We never substitute without your knowledge.

Pricing transparency

Your total = our service fee + the exact Publix receipt amount. We send you a photo of the real receipt after delivery. No estimated prices, no averages — you pay what the items actually cost at Publix that day.

Large orders

For orders where the grocery total exceeds $1,000, each $1,000 block incurs one service fee. This is communicated clearly before you confirm the order.

7. Data storage & security

Your profile and order history are stored in our secure database (Supabase — PostgreSQL, hosted on AWS). Conversation data is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI and is subject to Anthropic's privacy policy. We implement standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls.

8. Data retention & deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. To request deletion of your data, email dealsconnectllc@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request." We will process your request within 30 days.

9. Cookies & tracking

Our app uses minimal, essential cookies only — to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics that share your data with third parties.

10. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify active users by email of material changes. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
